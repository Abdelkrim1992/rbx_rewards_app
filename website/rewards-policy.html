1. Nature of RBX Coins

Virtual "RBX Coins" are in-app promotional tokens. They are NOT legal tender, fiat currency, cryptocurrency, or negotiable monetary instruments.

  • Coins possess zero real-world cash surrender value and cannot be withdrawn as direct cash, PayPal transfers, or bank deposits.
  • Coins are strictly non-transferable between accounts, devices, or users.
  • Coin balances and transaction ledgers are cryptographically validated by our Supabase Edge Functions (credit-coins, spend-coins) backed by Upstash Redis and Postgres Row-Level Security.

5. Step-by-Step Redemption Process

  1. Balance Check: The app verifies your current balance against the card cost (20,000 / 40,000 / 70,000 coins). If insufficient, a floating alert informs you of the exact coins required.
  2. Confirmation Dialog (_RedeemConfirmDialog): Prompts you to confirm deducting the exact coin cost for your selected card denomination.
  3. Edge Function Execution: Upon confirmation, the spend-coins Edge Function securely updates your Supabase balance and logs the redemption record.
  4. Success Popup (RedeemSuccessDialog): Congratulates you and verifies that your request has entered the fulfillment queue.
  5. Live Status Tracking: Your claim appears immediately in the Redemption History section of the app.
