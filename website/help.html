Complete In-App Mechanics Guide This guide covers the exact gameplay modes, streak formulas, wheel spin segments, and 5,000 XP level steps present in the RBX Rewards Flutter app.

1. Core Earning Pillars

As shown directly in your Profile Screen's Help & Support section, there are four primary avenues to earn virtual RBX Coins:

🕹️ 1. Mini-Games

5 arcade games (Tap Tap, Math Quiz, Flappy Jump, Flip Cards, Scratch Card) with independent daily coin caps.

📝 2. Offers & Surveys

Integrated offerwalls powered by Tapjoy (v14.4) and PubScale (v1.0) awarding up to 1,000 coins daily.

🔥 3. Daily Streaks & Chests

Consecutive 24h logins (100 coins on Day 7!) and 1,000 bonus coins inside the Mega Mystery Chest.

🎡 4. Spin the Wheel

Daily lucky wheel offering 5 to 20 coins and a 25 RBX JACKPOT segment.

5. Level & 5,000 XP Progression Formula

In [`lib/presentation/screens/profile_screen.dart`](file:///d:/rbx_rewards_app/lib/presentation/screens/profile_screen.dart), your level is dynamically calculated as:

Level = Math.floor(totalEarned / 5000) + 1;

Every 5,000 total earned coins automatically advances your profile to the next level (e.g. Level 1 at 0 XP, Level 2 at 5,000 XP, Level 3 at 10,000 XP). Your XP progress bar visually tracks how many coins remain until your next level milestone.
