Account & Data Deletion Request

Standard: Google Play & Apple Store Mandated
SLA: Completed within 7–30 days
Email: privacy@rbxrewards.app
🗑️
Your Right to be Forgotten In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), GDPR (Article 17), and CCPA, you have the full legal right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

1. Deletion Rights Overview

When you request account deletion, we purge all personally identifiable information, username history, device mappings, and game stats from our primary active databases.

⚠️
Irreversible Action Warning Account deletion is permanent. Once processed, your virtual RBX Coin balance, high score records, unlocked daily chests, and streak levels cannot be restored or refunded.

2. Option A: Self-Service Deletion via Mobile App

If you still have the RBX Rewards mobile app installed on your device, you can perform an immediate local data wipe:

  1. Open your device's Settings menu.
  2. Navigate to Apps > RBX Rewards > Storage & Cache.
  3. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.
  4. Uninstall the application from your phone.

This action instantly erases all locally cached SharedPreferences, SQLite/Hive caches, and stored preferences from your physical hardware.

4. Data Scope: What is Deleted vs Retained

Category Examples Action Taken
Profile Data Display name, chosen avatar, user UUID Permanently wiped from Supabase
Game Progress Flappy Jump high scores, streak day count, level/XP Permanently purged
Virtual Balance Unredeemed RBX Coin balance Permanently zeroed and archived
Device Mappings Ad ID pairings, push notification tokens Unlinked and deleted
Fulfilled Digital Codes Pin codes and timestamps of past deliveries Anonymized and retained for 12 months solely for tax, financial accounting, and chargeback defense

5. Processing Timelines & Confirmation

  • Acknowledgment: Within 48 hours of receipt.
  • Database Purge: Within 7 business days of verification.
  • Backup Cache Roll-Off: Offsite disaster recovery backups overwrite on a 30-day cyclical rolling schedule.

If you encounter any issues submitting this form, you may also email our Data Protection Team directly at privacy@rbxrewards.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
